# Odyss

Your library. Your way.

Odyss is an open-source platform for building, tracking, and owning your personal movie and TV-series library.

## Vision

Odyss aims to give users full control over their personal media library through a modern, customizable experience. Whether self-hosted or cloud-hosted, your library should feel truly yours.
